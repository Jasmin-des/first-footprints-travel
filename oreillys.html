O'Reilly's Rainforest Retreat

A peaceful Gold Coast Hinterland escape, where eucalyptus branches sway, mountain air feels clean, and the forest looks after you.

Why people linger here

O'Reilly's is ideal for slow travellers who want easy forest walks, reflective breaks and beautiful views without rushing from one stop to the next.

  • Scenic canopy walks and birdwatching spots
  • Comfortable cabins and nearby dining
  • Easy access to lookout points and gentler walking routes

Visit notes

Best time
Morning for cooler temperatures and birdlife
Ideal pace
2-3 nights
Best for
Families, friends and grandparents

What to do

  • Take the scenic walk through the rainforest
  • Watch for lyrebirds and other local wildlife
  • Enjoy a slow breakfast overlooking the treetops
  • Stay for sunset and listen to the forest settle

Plan a gentle day

Start with a laid-back breakfast, take a short walk at mid-morning, pause for lunch, and return to the lodge before sunset.
