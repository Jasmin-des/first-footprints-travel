Riverlife Adventures

A gentle Brisbane River experience with easy paddling, city views and the occasional chance to slow down and just watch the water.

Easy river time

Riverlife Adventures suits older travellers who want a calm, scenic activity with plenty of time for conversation, rest and a relaxed lunch afterwards.

  • Gentle paddling routes with easy pace
  • Views over the river and city skyline
  • Friendly guides and comfortable group arrangements

Visit notes

Best time
Late morning or sunset
Ideal pace
Short half-day outing
Best for
Shared outings with family and friends

Suggested rhythm

  • Meet for coffee and a calm start
  • Paddle to a scenic section of the river
  • Pause at a riverside cafe or picnic area
  • End with a quiet walk back beside the water

Good to know

Comfortable clothing, sunscreen and a hat are all that is needed. The pace is designed to feel easy, scenic and social.
