Yura Tours

A thoughtful local guide experience that lets travellers hear the stories of Country in a deeply human, open-hearted way.

Walk with Country

Yura Tours offers a gentle cultural walk through meaningful places and the stories that shape them, making space for reflection and conversation.

  • Guided cultural walk with Aboriginal leaders
  • Stories of Country, history and living practice
  • Comfortable, respectful group pacing

Visit notes

Best time
Morning light and cooler weather
Ideal pace
2-3 hours with breaks
Best for
Families, friends, curious travellers

Why it is memorable

  • It is personal, grounded and deeply local
  • It brings together story, landscape and belonging
  • It makes room for listening and processing together

Travel suggestion

Pair the walk with a café stop nearby and leave enough time for a slower lunch and shared conversation afterwards.
