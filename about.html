About

Our way of travelling.

First Footprints is a slow travel concept designed for older adults who love the company of family and friends and want journeys that feel generous, grounded and easy.

Why this website exists

We design for the people who do not need a packed itinerary. We design for the people who like a warm breakfast on the veranda, a bench with a view, a story told slowly, and a day that leaves room for rest.

First Footprints brings together rainforest, river and cultural experiences across Queensland in a way that feels warm, thoughtful and easy to navigate.

Website details

Target audience: adults aged 65+, travelling in family or friendship groups.
Focus: slow travel, comfort, accessibility, shared experiences.

References & inspiration

  • Visit Brisbane — First Nations experiences in Brisbane
  • Road Scholar travel archives and slow-travel storytelling
  • Scrapbook-inspired travel layouts and natural paper textures
  • Queensland nature and cultural destinations selected for comfort and accessibility

Plan your journey

Choose one rainforest stop, one river-based outing and one cultural experience, then leave space for quiet mornings and late lunches.
